How to play

Click the course to aim. Set power and swing, or pull back from the ball and release. Arrow keys adjust aim; Space swings when the course is focused. The dashed line shows your expected shot.

Sand and rough shorten shots. Water and out of bounds cost a penalty stroke and return your ball. Switch to the putter on the green. Lowest score wins.
