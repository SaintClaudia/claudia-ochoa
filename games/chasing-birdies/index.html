How to play

Tap the course to aim. Drag the target marker to adjust aim and power, or use the aim slider for small adjustments. Set power and press Swing. Pulling back from the ball also sets aim and power; enable swing on release here if you prefer that gesture. Arrow keys adjust aim; Space swings when the course is focused. The dashed line and landing preview show your expected shot before ball collisions.

Purdy has the least wind drift, McCaffrey keeps more distance from rough and sand, and Kittle hits the longest drives. Player traits are balanced for this game. Putting is the same for everyone. Club distances reflect your current lie. The suggested club and power fit the distance to the pin; adjust them for your shot. The driver covers long distances, the 7 iron handles approaches, and the sand wedge flies high and keeps more distance from bunkers. Low-power shots fly lower. Putting works best on the green; rough and sand sharply reduce its range. Sand and rough shorten shots. Water and out of bounds cost a penalty stroke and return your ball. Switch to the putter on the green. Lowest score wins.
