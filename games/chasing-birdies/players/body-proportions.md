# On-course body proportions

The active swing assets are `purdy-swing-v2.webp`, `mccaffrey-swing-v2.webp`, and `kittle-swing-v2.webp`. Player settings continue to use the original portrait assets.

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, editing each existing swing sheet. Transparent PNG outputs were converted to WebP at quality 88, preserving alpha. Each sheet has six poses and two outfit rows.

Relative heights use the [49ers roster](https://www.49ers.com/team/rosters): Purdy 73 inches / 220 lb, McCaffrey 71 inches / 210 lb, Kittle 76 inches / 250 lb. Silhouettes are artistic interpretations of their athletic builds. Source body heights are calibrated separately per outfit; the canvas preserves each sprite's aspect ratio. Address and impact clubhead anchors keep contact at the ball.
