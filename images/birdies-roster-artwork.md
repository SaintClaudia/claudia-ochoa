# Chasing Birdies concept roster

Approved portfolio artwork, November 5, 2026. Created with the built-in ImageGen
tool as a single 4-by-4 portrait atlas, followed by an apparel-logo edit. The
compressed production asset is `birdies-roster-golf-teams.jpg` (1086 × 1448).
This is concept art for the portfolio story, not photographs or new playable
characters in the separate game prototype.

## Generation brief

One 4-column by 4-row, equal-cell, edge-to-edge portrait atlas, overall 3:4 aspect
ratio. Near-photorealistic, lightly stylized sports-game characters in golf attire,
cropped at upper thighs with heads fully visible, relaxed poses, accurate distinct
faces and builds, natural editorial lighting, realistic fabric, consistent deep
forest-green studio backgrounds. No hats, football uniforms, captions, borders,
gutters, or UI. Exact row-major player order as above. Golf clothing colors by cell:
ivory, navy, sage, burgundy; ivory, slate blue, charcoal quarter zip, sand; light
blue, charcoal, ivory textured, olive; navy quarter zip, taupe, muted rust, pale
sage.

## Approved edit brief

Preserve all sixteen faces, hairstyles, poses, golf clothing, colors, crop,
lighting, background, and equal-cell atlas geometry. Change only the left chest
(viewer right): add a realistic embroidered current/last-team logo, about three
inches across, following the fabric, using the assignments above. Use the 49ers
SF oval, Buccaneers pirate flag, Bills charging buffalo, Chiefs arrowhead,
Cardinals head, Cowboys star, Steelers circular mark, Raiders shield, Rams LA
horn mark, Broncos horse head, and Jaguars head as appropriate. No other text,
sponsors, or clothing changes.
