# Player lineup artwork

The portfolio's player selection screen presents the seven players requested for the pitch. It is a static V1 presentation, with Brock Purdy selected; these cards do not expand the playable game's selectable roster.

Existing uniform portrait assets are reused for Brock Purdy, George Kittle, and Christian McCaffrey. New transparent portraits of Kyle Juszczyk, Brandon Allen, Fred Warner, and Nick Bosa were created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The generated portraits are visualizations, not official photographs. Each source was saved under `.codex/generated_images/01a13524-a5ee-7c81-b303-c06a45dbcb95/` and optimized to a 480-pixel PNG with its alpha channel preserved for the portfolio. All four production assets are in `images/birdies-*-uniform.png`.

Jersey numbers and positions were checked against the [official 49ers roster](https://www.49ers.com/team/rosters). Brandon Allen is deliberately depicted in his former 49ers number 17 uniform, matching the user's requested pitch lineup and the [NFL's 2024 49ers roster](https://www.nfl.com/sitemap/html/rosters/2024/san-francisco-49ers). This page does not claim that all seven are on the current roster.

## Portrait framing revision

The three original narrow uniform portraits were replaced only in the portfolio cards by `birdies-purdy-uniform-v2.png`, `birdies-kittle-uniform-v2.png`, and `birdies-mccaffrey-uniform-v2.png`. ImageGen rebuilt standalone portraits from the original character sheets, retaining the uniform character appearance while bringing both arms and hands fully inside the image. Bosa’s portrait was also cleaned into `birdies-bosa-uniform-v2.png` to remove stray objects. Original assets remain available, including those used by the playable game. The card renderer no longer masks either side of the portrait. New revisions are optimized to 600 pixels with alpha preserved.
