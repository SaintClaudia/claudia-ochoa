# Chasing Birdies wardrobe likeness

Updated November 5, 2026. The portfolio's “Draft and style” sequence uses three
matched cutouts with a consistent Brock Purdy likeness, relaxed stance, natural
athletic proportions, and soft daylight matching the newer swing presentation.

## Assets

- `images/birdies-purdy-cohesive-black.webp`: black 49ers polo, tan trousers,
  white shoes, and a white 13 glove on the left hand.
- `images/birdies-purdy-cohesive-ivory.webp`: ivory signature 13 polo, matching
  trousers and shoes, both hands bare.
- `images/birdies-purdy-cohesive-game-day.webp`: scarlet number 13 uniform,
  gold pants, white cleats, and a helmet held in the right hand.

All assets are 1024 × 1536 with transparency. The small player portrait crops
the black-polo master through an SVG viewBox, so it shares the same face without
another independently generated headshot. The existing scroll-driven outfit
sequence and wardrobe selections are preserved.

## Creation

Built-in image generation created the black-polo master from the official
49ers headshot and the approved swing starting frame. The ivory and football
versions were outfit-only edits of that master, preserving face, pose, framing,
and lighting. The transparent PNGs were encoded to WebP at quality 90; no
geometric or facial retouching was applied during export.

Official identity reference: https://www.49ers.com/team/players-roster/brock-purdy/

These are generated concept assets. Reference photos are not published assets.

## Brand mark correction

The ivory polo and worn golf glove use the supplied Brock Purdy 13 signature
mark from “Brock Purdy 13 Branding Moodboard.png.” The glove follows the
existing `images/birdies-glove-13.webp` catalog reference: smooth white leather,
simple wrist fastening, and the scarlet mark on the back of the hand. These were
localized edits of the approved cutouts. The football uniform, its athletic
numbering, and the black polo’s 49ers logo are unchanged.

The glove mark was subsequently reduced to approximately 75% of its previous
width and height, both on the worn glove and in its catalog thumbnail. Polo
branding and football numbering were preserved.
