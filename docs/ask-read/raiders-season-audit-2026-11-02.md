## Boundaries and unresolved discrepancies

- Evidence states describe recurring recorded outcomes, not a validated schematic cause. No finding is labeled Established without a complete film review.
- Jets recap completion totals conflict with its box score and NFL game log. Completion-based opponent rates are excluded.
- Some individual defensive player totals and logs do not reconcile with the team sack sum. Player sacks are reported as listed; no proportional share is calculated.

The team third-down total (40/96) is cross-checked against the NFL defensive downs table; it has not been reconstructed from every play. No down-and-distance inference is made.

All eight official inactive lists were reviewed; full defensive inactive arrays and per-game source URLs are in the accompanying JSON. No absence, elevation, or blank statistical line is used as proof of a specific replacement’s snaps.

The three featured preview images show two Jets first-half passing touchdowns and Davis Allen’s first-half Rams touchdown. They illustrate the recent scoring finding, not film verification of all ten findings. Other findings remain statistical or availability review items with linked evidence.
