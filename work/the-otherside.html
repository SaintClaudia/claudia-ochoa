The Otherside

Find your people.
Feel a little more at home.

Overview

A shared interest.
A real connection.

Finding your people can be the hardest part of moving somewhere new. The Otherside is a community app for people drawn to independent music and culture, creating a way to meet through the things they already love.

A record-shop visit. A small show. An afternoon of listening. Shared plans give the first conversation somewhere to begin, and a reason to meet again.

Strategic objectives

  1. 01.

    Make the first hello easier.

    Start with a shared activity so meeting someone feels natural, even when you arrive on your own.

  2. 02.

    Turn common ground into company.

    Bring people together around music, places, and interests, with small groups that leave room for conversation.

  3. 03.

    Help a new place feel familiar.

    Connect newcomers and locals through repeat gatherings that can grow into friendships.

The experience

Something to do.
Someone to go with.

Browse by interest, see what a gathering is like, and save a plan. A host introduction, group size, and clear meeting details make the first step easier to picture.

The starting point

From relocating
to belonging.

I first explored this idea as Connect when I started at Walmart, thinking about people moving to work onsite as teams returned to the office. The need was personal and practical: how do you build a life and find friends in a new place?

The Otherside brings that idea to the same independent-minded audience as Nebulae. Music is the starting point; friendship and a sense of community are the purpose. This is a new concept exploration, not a launched Walmart product.

Designed for connection

Your pace.
Your kind of company.

The direction favors small gatherings in public places, interest-led introductions, and choosing when to connect. A future service would include host profiles, member controls, and clear reporting tools.

Nebulae helps you find the music. The Otherside helps you find the people who get it.
