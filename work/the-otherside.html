The Otherside

Not your mother’s social network.
A world beyond your usual circle.

Overview

In good company.

The Otherside is a place for creative minds. Discover what's new, see who's playing, try something worth exploring, or share something of your own. Whether you're already part of the scene or curious about a new spot, there's always another side to explore.

Strategic objectives

  1. 01.

    Make room for expression.

    Give artists, musicians, designers, and creative minds a space to share what they create, express their perspectives, and reach audiences beyond their existing circles.

  2. 02.

    Let curiosity lead.

    Make discovering independent music, art, and creative perspectives feel natural, introducing people to new interests and ideas they might not otherwise encounter.

  3. 03.

    Go beyond the feed.

    Bring independent culture into the real world through curated shows, exhibitions, local discoveries, and meetups that invite people to explore wherever they are.

The Feed

A different perspective.

Discover music, art, and ideas through the people sharing them. From emerging artists to established creatives, the feed brings different voices and perspectives into one ever-changing experience.
