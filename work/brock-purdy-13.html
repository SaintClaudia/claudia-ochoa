Brock Purdy 13

A higher standard in every detail. A fragrance identity shaped by discipline, confidence and balance.

↓

Overview

A number you can wear.

Brock Purdy 13 introduces a new expression of personal style through a five-piece fragrance and grooming collection. Built around his signature number, the collection extends a familiar identity into something more personal, creating a connection with an audience that knows him both on and off the field.

Conceived as a complete line, the collection imagines how an athlete’s presence might carry into everyday life. An eau de parfum sits at its center, with essentials for the shower and shave around it. Deep tones, rich textures, and the 13 mark tie each piece together, making the line as recognizable on a counter as the number is on the field.

Strategic objectives

  1. 01.

    Build a brand people connect with.

    Translate the admiration and affinity surrounding Brock Purdy into a fragrance collection that feels personal, aspirational, and relevant to everyday life.

  2. 02.

    Give people a reason to choose the collection.

    Make the fragrance and grooming products desirable in their own right, with an appeal that reaches devoted fans and people discovering Brock for the first time.

  3. 03.

    Create a brand built to grow.

    Establish a distinctive brand across fragrance, grooming, and packaging, with the flexibility to grow into future products and experiences.

The mark

More than a number.

The 1 and 3 come together in a single gesture, subtly forming a B. It’s a familiar number with a personal detail tucked inside — Brock’s first initial, revealed on a closer look.

From the fragrance bottle to the tag on a towel, the mark gives every piece a signature of its own.

The ritual is everything.

Time in the locker room gives you a feel for what works. Brock Purdy 13 brings that same attention to the everyday routine, from a close shave to the soft, quick-drying comfort that follows. Whether you’re getting ready for the day or washing it away, there’s comfort in a ritual that matters.

By Design

Beyond the uniform.

Brock Purdy 13 is a concept for a high-performance grooming and fragrance line that brings the care of game day into everyday life. Created around Brock Purdy and made for the people who follow him, the collection moves through the full rhythm of a day, from the morning shower to stepping out for the evening.

Whatever you put on for the day, the preparation beneath it is yours. Brock Purdy 13 turns that preparation into rituals that help you feel ready for what comes next.
