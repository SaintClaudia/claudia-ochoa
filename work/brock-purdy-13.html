Work / Fragrance

Brock Purdy 13

A higher standard in every detail. A fragrance identity shaped by discipline, confidence and balance.

↓

Overview

A signature, made tangible.

Brock Purdy 13 explores an expression of personal identity through fragrance. The number becomes the signature: a mark that carries from the bottle to the wider visual world.

Brand identity, packaging and art direction come together around a simple idea. Confidence can be expressed through restraint, with every detail working toward a coherent whole.

Strategic objectives

  1. 01.

    Create a distinctive identity.

    Make the 13 recognizable on its own, with a personal character that extends beyond a name.

  2. 02.

    Make every detail belong.

    Connect the mark, typography, finishes and bottle into one considered expression.

  3. 03.

    Build a world around the product.

    Use light, material and setting to communicate the same confidence as the identity.

The mark

One number. A personal signature.

The 1 and 3 are drawn as one gesture. The forward movement gives the mark energy, while the compact silhouette gives it a clear place on the bottle.

The mark leads. The name supports it. A restrained typographic hierarchy gives each element room to be recognized.

The bottle

Presence through restraint.

A dark glass body and substantial black cap establish the silhouette. A warm metal collar introduces a precise point of contrast.

The face stays quiet: the 13, Brock Purdy’s name, and the eau de parfum designation. The product’s form and reflections carry the rest.

Art direction

Light reveals the detail.

Golden light moves across textured stone, meeting the dark glass with sharp reflections. The setting brings warmth and depth to an otherwise restrained palette.

Space around the bottle keeps the composition focused. Light draws attention to the silhouette, the collar and the signature.

Design

Dark glass. Warm metal. Quiet type.

Black and deep brown ground the identity. Bronze and warm ivory provide contrast. The expressive signature sits against widely spaced lettering, balancing character with clarity.
