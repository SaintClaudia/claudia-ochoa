Brock Purdy 13

A higher standard in every detail. A fragrance identity shaped by discipline, confidence and balance.

↓

Overview

Beyond the signature.

Brock Purdy 13 introduces a new expression of personal style through a five-piece fragrance and grooming collection. Built around his signature number, the collection extends a familiar identity into something more personal, creating a connection with an audience that knows him both on and off the field.

From the fragrance itself to the packaging and visual identity, every element contributes to a unified brand experience. The result is a collection designed to stand on its own, with an identity that feels distinctly Brock Purdy.

Strategic objectives

  1. 01.

    Build a brand people connect with.

    Translate the admiration and affinity surrounding Brock Purdy into a fragrance collection that feels personal, aspirational, and relevant to everyday life.

  2. 02.

    Connect through shared interests.

    Connect with football fans, aspiring athletes, and new consumers through a product that brings together personal style and a familiar sporting identity.

  3. 03.

    Create a brand built to grow.

    Establish a distinctive brand across fragrance, grooming, and packaging, with the flexibility to grow into future products and experiences.

The mark

One number. A personal signature.

The 1 and 3 are drawn as one gesture. The forward movement gives the mark energy, while the compact silhouette gives it a clear place on the bottle.

The mark leads. The name supports it. A restrained typographic hierarchy gives each element room to be recognized.

The ritual is everything.

There’s something about feeling refreshed, putting on a familiar scent, and being ready for whatever comes next.

Design

Grounded in sophistication.
Refined by design.

A warm, understated palette inspired by natural stone, soft textiles, and rich wood tones. Oatmeal and limestone bring warmth, while deep espresso and charcoal provide contrast. Together, the materials create a sense of quiet sophistication that feels masculine without being imposing.
