Nebulae

Music for those that
seek independence.

Overview

For us by us.

Nebulae is a lossless music streaming service built around independent music and the subculture that surrounds it. Bringing genres like psych, garage, doom, and synth to the forefront.

Shaped by the artists, creatives, rebels, and independent thinkers who make up this culture, Nebulae brings the music and the people it moves closer together. A place to find the sounds you love, discover something new, or immerse yourself in what inspires you.

Because discovering music is only part of the experience. What it inspires is just as important.

Strategic objectives

  1. 01.

    Make discovery organic.

    Bring subgenres to the forefront through curated radio that makes discovering new music feel effortless, whether actively exploring or simply listening while creating.

  2. 02.

    Give independent music greater visibility.

    Create opportunities for artists and labels to be discovered by listeners who already connect with their sound, without relying solely on traditional promotion.

  3. 03.

    Keep the scene within reach.

    Make it easier to stay connected to the artists and bands you love, from following new releases to discovering where they're playing next.

The vision

Time lapse.

Nebulae first took shape in 2016, with an original identity, product design, and a vision for independent music that extended beyond streaming. From the beginning, the idea was to bring underrepresented genres to the forefront, give independent artists and labels greater visibility, and connect listeners to music both on and off the stage.

The vision remains much as it was originally imagined. What has changed is the landscape around it. As record stores, independent venues, and local music scenes face new challenges, finding and staying connected to independent music takes on greater importance.

What began as an idea nearly a decade ago feels more necessary today than ever.

Brand identity

An independent universe.

After years of creating identities for other companies, Nebulae became an opportunity to explore my own. A creative space without predetermined rules, where I could experiment freely and build something rooted in my personal influences and the independent music culture I love.

The identity began with a geometric interpretation of the traditional triangle and eye, drawing inspiration from the concept of illumination. Its name references the cosmic clouds where stars are born, connecting ideas of exploration, discovery, and new beginnings.

That spirit carries through the visual language, from the simplicity of the iconography to the more expressive artwork and playful use of words. Every element became an opportunity to bring personality into the experience, creating a world that feels as independent as the music it celebrates.
