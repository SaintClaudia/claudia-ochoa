Overview

Independent music.
A culture of its own.

Indie has always meant more than a category. It’s the records passed between friends. The small room with the unforgettable set. The artists making something because they have to.

Nebulae brings that spirit into music discovery, connecting listeners with independent labels, self-releasing artists, and radio stations that open up unfamiliar sounds.

Strategic objectives

  1. 01.

    Make discovery personal.

    Use curated stations to connect listeners with scenes, moods, and sounds beyond familiar categories.

  2. 02.

    Make room for independence.

    Give label communities and self-releasing artists a place in the same listening experience.

  3. 03.

    Bring listeners closer.

    Carry forward the original vision for lossless listening and discovering bands on tour.

The original vision

Rooted in a real music community.

The original Nebulae idea grew out of a love for independent music and work with labels including Merge, Goner, Dead Oceans, and Matador.

A2IM featured Nebulae in its December 12, 2016 Associate Member Spotlight. The original vision paired lossless streaming with a dedicated space for promoting bands on tour.

This new exploration brings that history forward, with room for both label communities and self-releasing artists. Historical label relationships do not represent current partnerships or a licensed catalog.

Brand identity

An independent universe.

The original triangle-and-record mark anchors the identity. Quiet cosmic artwork creates a sense of space around the music, with soft violet light and deep, restrained tones.
