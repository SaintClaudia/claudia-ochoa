Common questions

Everything you need to know.

Orders & shipping

How long does shipping take?

Most orders ship within 3–5 business days, with a delivery window confirmed by email and text as your order gets closer.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, as long as it hasn't shipped yet. Contact us with your order number and we'll take care of it.

How do I check my order status?

Use Order Status from the account menu, or reach out and we'll look it up for you.

Returns & warranty

What's your return policy?

Every Sactionals piece comes with a 60-day trial. Not feeling it? We'll help arrange a return.

What does the warranty cover?

Frames, connectors, and foam are covered against defects for the lifetime of the product. Fabric coverage varies by material.

How do I start a warranty claim?

Message us with your order number and a few photos, and we'll walk you through next steps.

Assembly & care

Do I need tools to assemble it?

No tools required — every piece snaps together by hand. See what to expect for a full walkthrough.

How do I clean the covers?

Most covers unzip and are machine washable on cold, gentle cycle. Check the care tag for your specific fabric.

Can I reconfigure my setup later?

That's the point of Sactionals — rearrange pieces or add more as your space changes.
