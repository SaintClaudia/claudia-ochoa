Ask Read

Every Sunday starts on Monday. Ask Read turns a week of opponent film into a short briefing a quarterback can trust, with the plays behind every point.

↓

Overview

Film study, simplified for the quarterback.

A quarterback’s week runs on film. Hours of opponent tape, spread across clips, notes and conversations, have to become a handful of decisions by Sunday. Ask Read uses computer vision to break down the opponent and turns what it finds into a briefing written for one reader: the quarterback. It leads with what matters most, shows how strong the evidence is, and keeps the plays one tap away.

Strategic objectives

  1. 01.

    Put the few things that matter most this week first.

  2. 02.

    Back every finding with the plays that support it.

  3. 03.

    Be honest about certainty, and say so when the film isn’t enough.

01

The weekly briefing

Each week opens with the opponent distilled to what matters most, sized to read in minutes rather than hours.

02

Four ways in

Opportunity, Pressure, Coverage and Personnel. Switch between them directly, and see how many key points sit behind each finding.

03

The film behind it

Every finding links to the clips that support it, labeled by quarter, down and concept, so the evidence is never more than a tap away.

04

Ask Read anything

Questions in plain language, kept alongside the week. When the film can’t support an answer, Ask Read says so instead of guessing.

Innovation

Computer vision that shows its work.

Ask Read separates what is visible on film from what it means. A detected alignment is an observation; calling it a coverage is an interpretation that has to be earned. Findings reach the briefing only after they are checked against counterevidence, and the model’s confidence is never presented as football certainty.

Each finding carries a plain evidence state, so a quarterback knows at a glance how much weight to give it.

Design

Calm, warm and built for focus.

A near-black foundation, a single champagne accent and one typeface, Outfit, keep attention on the football. Containers are kept to a minimum: type, spacing and a few dividers do the grouping, so findings read as information rather than chat.
