Ask Read

Every Sunday starts on Monday. Ask Read transforms game film and performance data into actionable quarterback intelligence.

↓

Overview

Film study, simplified for the quarterback.

A quarterback’s week runs on film. Hours of opponent tape, spread across clips, notes and conversations, have to become a handful of decisions by Sunday. Ask Read uses computer vision to break down the opponent and turns what it finds into a briefing written for one reader: the quarterback. It leads with what matters most, shows how strong the evidence is, and keeps the plays one tap away.

Strategic objectives

  1. 01.

    Reduce cognitive overload.

    Surface the most relevant intelligence so quarterbacks can focus on what matters most each week.

  2. 02.

    Accelerate game preparation.

    Connect information across games to reveal meaningful tendencies and reduce the time required to find them.

  3. 03.

    Strengthen quarterback decision‑making.

    Deliver contextual intelligence about opponents and their own team to support preparation, anticipation, and execution.

01

The brief

Each week, Ask Read brings forward the most relevant intelligence on the opponent, connecting patterns and surfacing emerging tendencies in a briefing designed to be read in minutes, not hours.

02

The opponent

Ask Read connects previous with current performances, analyzing defensive tendencies, personnel changes, and patterns across games to deliver a comprehensive understanding of the opponent and what matters most for that week's matchup.

03

The film

Ask Read connects intelligence to film by understanding the plays and delivering only what the quarterback needs to know. Each finding allows the quarterback to explore the evidence, gain a deeper understanding of the opponent, and make more informed decisions. A dedicated workspace brings all game film together in one place for seamless viewing and analysis.

04

The ask

Ask Read makes intelligence conversational, allowing quarterbacks to ask questions and explore their next opponent with answers grounded in evidence. Over time, it learns from their interests and adapts future briefings to anticipate their needs as their preparation evolves.

Plays

Intelligence meets the playbook.

Ask Read connects opponent tendencies with offensive concepts to identify opportunities worth exploring. Each suggestion brings together the play, supporting evidence, and matchup considerations, giving quarterbacks and coaches the intelligence to evaluate their options.

Library

The season stays with you.

Preparation doesn’t disappear when the week is over. The Library keeps past briefings, the questions you asked, Read’s responses, and the asks you saved, organized by week and season. Switch to Offseason to revisit self-scout, OTAs, and minicamp.

Return to an earlier matchup and pick up the thread. The findings and their film references stay with the original briefing, so you can revisit what you studied in the context that made it matter.

Innovation

Intelligence you can trust.

Ask Read uses computer vision to analyze game film, identifying patterns, tendencies, and opportunities across plays. Its AI architecture is designed to ensure insights are grounded in evidence, evaluated for accuracy, and communicated with clarity.

More than an intelligence platform, Ask Read is intelligent software that adapts to the quarterback. It continuously prioritizes what matters most, bringing forward the most relevant information based on the opponent, game context, and evolving needs of the quarterback.

The result is a dynamic intelligence experience that moves beyond delivering information to anticipating what matters next.
