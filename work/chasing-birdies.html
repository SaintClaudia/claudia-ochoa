Overview

A different kind of playing field.

What happens when the players you cheer for on Sunday step onto the green? Chasing Birdies brings Brock Purdy and company into a golf simulation where the setting changes, but the competitive spirit stays.

The experience begins with a familiar face and opens into a bigger world: iconic courses, changing conditions, and the satisfaction of finding the right shot. A game built around the feeling of being there, with football’s familiar faces along for the round.

Strategic objectives

  1. 01.

    Make fandom playable.

    Bring the personalities fans already love into an experience they can take part in.

  2. 02.

    Make every course a destination.

    Let the landscape set the mood and shape the round, from the first tee to the final putt.

  3. 03.

    Make the next shot matter.

    Build the tension around reading the course, choosing a shot, and committing to it.

By Design

The bigger picture.

Chasing Birdies is more than an idea for a golf game. It's a vision for an immersive simulation that brings together the precision of the sport, the personalities of professional athletes, and the excitement of competition.

Grounded in realistic course environments and gameplay mechanics, the experience leaves room for something unexpected. Whether stepping onto the green in traditional golf attire or full football gear, players can embrace the competitive nature of golf while exploring a more playful side of the sport.

At its heart, Chasing Birdies celebrates the love of golf shared by the athletes who have made football great. It invites fans to trade the football field for the fairway and join their favorite players in a game they love, one birdie at a time.
