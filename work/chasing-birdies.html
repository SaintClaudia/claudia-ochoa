Overview

A new way to play with the Niners.

What happens when the players you cheer for on Sunday step onto the green? Chasing Birdies brings Brock Purdy and company into a golf simulation where the setting changes, but the competitive spirit stays.

The experience begins with a familiar face and opens into a bigger world: iconic courses, changing conditions, and the satisfaction of finding the right shot. A game built around the feeling of being there, with the Niners along for the round.

THE PLAYERS

Familiar faces.
New rivalries.

The San Francisco 49ers bring their love of golf to you. Their chemistry is undeniable, and their dynamic takes on a whole new dimension as teammates become rivals and friends become fierce competitors. Dive into the fun, experience the camaraderie, and expect the unexpected as these gridiron stars chase the perfect swing.

Creative innovation

Some plays deserve another round.

The game doesn’t end with the final whistle. It finds its way to the fairway, where the stakes are different, and there’s more than one way to settle the score.

That shared passion sparked the idea behind Chasing Birdies. From professional athletes to the people cheering them on, football and golf bring together a community that thrives on competition, camaraderie, and a little friendly rivalry. It’s a connection that already exists, and an opportunity to turn that shared enthusiasm into something more.

Chasing Birdies brings those worlds together in a new kind of golf experience. Choose your player, challenge your friends, and bring your own rivalries to the course. Because the competition was never just about who you’re cheering for. It’s about getting in the game yourself.

Design

A world you want to step into.

Deep greens, warm gold, and panoramic course views give Chasing Birdies a sense of place. The Niners’ scarlet brings the players into that world, while quiet interface details keep the course at the center.

The experience should feel inviting before the first swing, focused during the shot, and worth coming back to after the round.

Development

Beyond the concept.

Chasing Birdies is more than an idea for a golf game. It's a vision for an immersive simulation that brings together the precision of the sport, the personalities of professional athletes, and the excitement of competition.

From realistic course environments and gameplay mechanics to player interactions and competitive formats, the experience has been envisioned with a foundation that can extend into a fully realized product.

The opportunity is to bring that vision to life through the right combination of creative direction, technology, and collaboration.
