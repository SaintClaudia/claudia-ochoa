Overview

A new way to play with the Niners.

What happens when the players you cheer for on Sunday step onto the first tee? Chasing Birdies brings Purdy, McCaffrey, and Kittle into a golf simulation where the setting changes, but the competitive spirit stays.

The experience begins with a familiar face and opens into a bigger world: iconic courses, changing conditions, and the satisfaction of finding the right shot. A game built around the feeling of being there, with the Niners along for the round.

The ambition

  1. 01.

    Make fandom playable.

    Bring the personalities fans already love into an experience they can take part in.

  2. 02.

    Make every course a destination.

    Let the landscape set the mood and shape the round, from the first tee to the final putt.

  3. 03.

    Make the next shot matter.

    Build the tension around reading the course, choosing a shot, and committing to it.

01 / THE PLAYERS

Familiar faces.
Fresh rivalries.

Purdy’s composure. McCaffrey’s versatility. Kittle’s power. Their identities carry into the game, giving each player a different way to approach the same course.

02 / THE WORLD

The course
calls the shots.

The coast at Pebble Beach. The open links at St. Andrews. Each destination brings its own rhythm, its own conditions, and a new reason to tee it up again.

03 / THE ROUND

Read the wind.
Trust the swing.

Distance is only the beginning. The lie, the wind, and the landing shape the decision. Play it safe or go for the pin—the next shot is yours.

Creative innovation

The connection is the starting point.

Fans already know these players. Chasing Birdies builds on that connection and gives it somewhere new to go. The joy is seeing familiar personalities in a different setting, then discovering how they change the way you play.

The brand, the world, and the mechanics tell the same story. The Niners have left the stadium. The competition has come with them.

Design

A world you want to step into.

Deep greens, warm gold, and panoramic course views give Chasing Birdies a sense of place. The Niners’ scarlet brings the players into that world, while quiet interface details keep the course at the center.

The experience should feel inviting before the first swing, focused during the shot, and worth coming back to after the round.

Development

The first swing is already playable.

I built a playable sketch to explore the heart of the game: choosing a player, reading a hole, and making a shot. Wind, different lies, club selection, and turn-by-turn competition bring those decisions to life.

That first round is the foundation for the larger experience. The vision reaches beyond it; the work starts by making the game feel good to play.
