Pump Up the Volume

Calling this my agentic browsing beta test — analog mode, crash out included.

Claudia Ochoa September 9, 2026 · 2 min read
01

I searched "body wash" in incognito mode 😉 just to see.

First result was my pick.

I know how this works. Ad auctions, bidding, SEO, the whole machine behind the curtain. Understanding the mechanism doesn't make it less wild that all it takes is two words to get an opportunity to experience the matrix.

It wasn't on Amazon. I wanted speed, not a deep dive into a brand site, so I ordered it for pickup at Ulta instead. The in-store experience was not my cup of tea, and when I got home the bottle was already opened — and later I would find out mid-shower the pump didn't work.

I stewed. ngl I crashed out. To the person who received my survey I meant what I said.

Then I used it the next day.

The world came to life.

A brand I had zero history with. Nothing about this required AI — I could've stumbled into this exact experience a decade ago. But something about doing it this way, watching the algorithm make a choice on my behalf and then getting proven right by the actual product, felt different.

It's agentic. Not just the search. The scent, the whole sequence — something acted, and I felt it.

I can speak. I can see. I can create.
But most of all, I can feel you.
