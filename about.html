About

Human-centered AI, shaped by business insight.

My perspective

I'm an AI experience and product design leader with 20+ years of experience helping companies — including Walmart — turn complex systems into experiences people can understand, trust, and use.

My leadership starts with the problem, not the technology. I connect customer needs, business value, brand, and delivery so AI has a clear role, a measurable purpose, and a human experience around it. At Walmart, that approach shaped a conversational GenAI hiring concept, earned early leadership support, and positioned me as Creative Director for the initiative.

I bring the same systems view to product and customer experience: research the real friction, decide where technology adds value, make risk and limitations visible, and define how success should be measured. My MBA from Purdue Global strengthens the business side of that work, and my JD studies at Purdue Global Law School are expanding the legal fluency I bring to emerging-technology decisions.

My mission
“To make emerging technology useful, trustworthy, and human — then connect it to business value.”
How I lead

Clear direction. Shared ownership.

The strongest work happens when people understand the problem, the trade-offs, and the part they own. My role is to create that clarity, connect the disciplines around it, and keep the human experience intact as the work moves forward.

01 · Frame

Start with the problem.

Define the human need and business outcome before choosing the technology, so teams align around purpose instead of novelty.

02 · Align

Build a direction others can use.

Translate complexity into a clear experience vision that leaders, partners, and delivery teams can understand, challenge, and move forward together.

03 · Decide

Make trade-offs visible.

Name uncertainty, risk, success measures, and human handoffs early. Good collaboration depends on clarity about what the system should—and should not—do.

04 · Deliver

Hold the vision without holding all the work.

Stay accountable for the experience through delivery while giving specialists room to shape the solution and making ownership clear across the system.
