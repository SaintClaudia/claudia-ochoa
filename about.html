My perspective

I'm a brand and UX strategist with 20+ years of experience helping companies — including Fortune 500 names like Walmart — connect with customers through design that actually understands people.

My work sits at the intersection of branding, marketing, and user experience: finding the emotional gap in a customer journey and closing it with design that feels intentional, not just functional. That's the lens I brought to my recent Lovesac homepage redesign concept — a case study in what happens when transparency and empathy get built into the buying experience from the start.

I recently completed my MBA at Purdue Global, and I'm continuing on to a JD at Purdue Global Law School. I'm building toward a future as a CEO in tech — one where design thinking, business strategy, and legal fluency come together to lead differently.
