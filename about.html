My perspective

I'm an AI experience and product design leader with 20+ years of experience helping companies — including Walmart — turn complex systems into experiences people can understand, trust, and use.

My leadership starts with the problem, not the technology. I connect customer needs, business value, brand, and delivery so AI has a clear role, a measurable purpose, and a human experience around it. At Walmart, that approach shaped a conversational GenAI hiring concept, earned early leadership support, and positioned me as Creative Director for the initiative.

I bring the same systems view to product and customer experience: research the real friction, decide where technology adds value, make risk and limitations visible, and define how success should be measured. My MBA from Purdue Global strengthens the business side of that work, and my JD studies at Purdue Global Law School are expanding the legal fluency I bring to emerging-technology decisions.
